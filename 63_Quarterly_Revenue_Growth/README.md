# 📈 Day 63: Quarterly Revenue Growth Analysis
![Cover](../images/125.png)

### 🎯 Objective
Measuring the financial growth trajectory of the company by calculating the percentage change in revenue between reporting quarters.

### 💼 Accounting Context
* **Investor Relations:** A key metric reported to shareholders to demonstrate business expansion.
* **Trend Analysis (CMA):** Essential for understanding revenue cycles and seasonality in financial planning.

### 📗 Excel Approach
**Formula:** `=(B3 / B2) - 1`

### 🐍 Python Approach
**Logic:**
Using `.pct_change()` to instantly analyze growth across multiple years of data, providing a scalable solution for corporate financial reporting.
