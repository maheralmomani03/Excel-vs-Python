# 📈 Day 31: Marketing Campaign ROI Calculator
![Cover](../images/61.png)

### 🎯 Objective
Measuring the efficiency of marketing spend by calculating the Return on Investment (ROI) for various campaigns. This helps in identifying which channels provide the best financial performance.

### 💼 Accounting Context
* **Marketing Accountability:** Justifying marketing budgets to the CFO by showing clear financial returns.
* **Resource Allocation:** Optimizing the advertising mix based on objective profitability metrics.

### 📗 Excel Approach
**Formula:** `=(B2 - C2) / C2`
**Logic:** Direct percentage calculation of profit relative to cost.

### 🐍 Python Approach
**Logic:** Scalable ROI analysis that can handle complex attribution models and thousands of campaign rows instantly.
